# VeriESG 🌱

### AI-Powered Sustainability & ESG Evidence Analyzer

> **AI-powered evidence for a sustainable future.**
> Transforming ESG compliance and reporting with intelligent, data-driven insights.

VeriESG is an AI-powered ESG analysis platform designed to help organizations, auditors, investors, and compliance teams analyze sustainability reports, identify unsupported ESG claims, uncover disclosure gaps, and generate audit-ready evidence.

---

## 🚀 Why VeriESG?

ESG reporting is becoming increasingly important for companies, investors, lenders, regulators, and customers.

However, ESG reports contain thousands of claims across:

- 🌱 Environmental performance
- 👥 Social responsibility
- 🏛️ Corporate governance
- 📊 Sustainability disclosures
- 📑 Regulatory compliance

Manually reviewing these claims is time-consuming and makes it difficult to determine whether reported information is actually supported by evidence.

**VeriESG solves this problem using AI-driven evidence analysis.**

---

## 🎯 What VeriESG Does

Upload an ESG report in PDF format and VeriESG analyzes the document to produce structured, evidence-based insights.

### Key Outputs

#### 📊 1. ESG Scorecard

Provides:

- Overall ESG score
- Environmental score
- Social score
- Governance score

#### 🔎 2. Evidence-to-Claim Matrix

Maps individual ESG claims to their supporting evidence.

Each claim includes:

- ESG category
- Supporting evidence
- Evidence strength
- Source/page reference

Evidence strength is classified as:

`High` | `Medium` | `Low`

#### ⚠️ 3. Greenwashing Risk Register

Identifies potentially problematic ESG statements, including:

- Unsupported claims
- Vague sustainability language
- Weak evidence
- Potential greenwashing risks

Each identified risk is assigned an appropriate risk level.

#### 📋 4. Disclosure Gap Analysis

Identifies missing or incomplete sustainability disclosures and relevant reporting frameworks, including frameworks such as:

- GRI
- TCFD

#### 🛠️ 5. Improvement Roadmap

Provides:

- Immediate corrective actions
- Long-term improvement opportunities
- Sustainability strategy recommendations

---

## 🇮🇳 Built for India's ESG Landscape

VeriESG is designed with India's ESG reporting environment in mind.

The platform focuses on **BRSR (Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report)** requirements and India's sustainability reporting ecosystem.

SEBI oversees the securities market and ESG reporting requirements for listed companies, while BRSR provides a structured framework for sustainability disclosures.

VeriESG can analyze BRSR filings, identify potential greenwashing risks, and benchmark sustainability performance against relevant indicators.
